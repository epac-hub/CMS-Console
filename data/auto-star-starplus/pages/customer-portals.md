> Scraped via Apify (Website Content Crawler) on 2026-07-28

The authoritative Star-Plus manuals, release notes, and help files are hosted behind two customer-only portals. Apify scrapes confirmed both are login-gated; their public-facing content is recorded below.

## 1. Auto-Star Customer Login (auto-star.com/login/)

> Source: https://www.auto-star.com/login/

A WordPress login (with a Nine10/OpenID Connect admin option). Fields: Username or Email Address, Password, Remember Me, plus a "Lost your password?" link (https://www.auto-star.com/wp-login.php?action=lostpassword).

Per Auto-Star's support pages, this login area provides customers with the latest product manuals, release notes, approved hardware lists, webinars, and download files.
