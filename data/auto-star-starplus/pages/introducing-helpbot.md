## What Is HelpBot?

HelpBot is a virtual AI powered assistant that provides instant answers to your Star-Plus questions. Instead of flipping through pages of documentation or waiting for support, you can now simply type your question into the "Ask HelpBot" field and get an immediate response.

HelpBot doesn't just provide a quick answer, it also shows the relevant source documentation, helping you understand the "why" behind the answer. By streamlining access to support information, HelpBot improves workflow efficiency and reduces downtime.

## How Your AI Powered Assistant Works

Using advanced AI capabilities, HelpBot scans the extensive Star-Plus documentation library to find the best possible answer to your query. Here's a step-by-step overview of how you can use it:

1.  **Type Your Query**: Enter your question into the "Ask HelpBot" text field.
2.  **Get Instant Results**: Press Enter and HelpBot will immediately return an answer based on the most relevant documentation.
3.  **Access Source Materials**: HelpBot includes links to the exact sections in the manuals, release notes, or technical tables where it found the answer.
4.  **Explore Follow-Up Questions**: Not quite done? HelpBot will suggest related follow-up questions to guide your next steps.

HelpBot supports complex queries and helps users at all technical levels better understand the Star-Plus ecosystem.

## Secure and Configurable Access

HelpBot access is governed by Star-Plus's **Trust Level** system. Through the Trust Level Editor, you can define which team members can use HelpBot, ensuring the right level of access across departments.

## A Note on AI Powered Assistant Limitations and Advice

As with any AI-powered assistant, it's important to recognize that HelpBot is continuously learning. While we strive for accuracy, there may be occasional surprises or mistakes. If something seems unclear or off, users are encouraged to consult traditional support or report the issue so we can improve the system.

HelpBot Tips:

1.  Talk to the AI using full sentences for best results, rather than short 2 or 3 word phrases. "How do I enable Penny Rounding" would usually get better results than typing "penny rounding".
2.  Where possible, state the program. E.g. "How do I add a customer in Customer Editor" will give better results than "How do I add a customer".
